MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on June 12, 2025 between Fernhill Robotics Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Fernhill"), and Ostrava Precision Castings s.r.o., a company organized under the laws of the Czech Republic ("Ostrava"). Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other, and each is a "Disclosing Party" for what it discloses and a "Receiving Party" for what it receives.

WHEREAS the parties are evaluating a possible collaboration in which Ostrava would cast aluminium housings for Fernhill's warehouse picking robots (the "Purpose");

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties hereby agree as follows.

1\. Confidential Information.

1.1 Definition. "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party in connection with the Purpose, in any form, including drawings, tolerances, alloy specifications, cost models, production volumes and the existence of these discussions.

1.2 Marking. Information disclosed orally or visually is Confidential Information only if the Disclosing Party identifies it as confidential at the time of disclosure and summarizes it in writing within fifteen (15) days.

2\. Obligations of the Receiving Party.

2.1 Use. The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and shall not use it to design, cast or source any competing product.

2.2 Disclosure to Representatives. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to its employees, officers and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than this Agreement. The Receiving Party is liable for any breach by those persons.

2.3 Standard of Care. The Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses for its own information of similar importance, and in no event less than reasonable care.

2.4 No Reverse Engineering. The Receiving Party shall not analyse, scan or reverse engineer any prototype, sample casting or software provided by the Disclosing Party, except as expressly needed for the Purpose and agreed in writing.

3\. Exclusions.

3.1 The obligations in Section 2 do not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession before disclosure, as shown by its written records;

(c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to the Confidential Information; or

(d) is received from a third party who is free to disclose it.

3.2 Required by Law. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or order of a court, provided that it gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice, where permitted, and cooperates with any effort to obtain confidential treatment.

4\. Term and Return of Information.

4.1 Term. This Agreement applies to disclosures made during two (2) years from its date. The obligations of confidentiality survive for five (5) years after the last disclosure, and for trade secrets for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

4.2 Return or Destruction. Within ten (10) business days after the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify the destruction in writing, except for copies kept in routine electronic backups, which remain subject to this Agreement.

5\. No Licence and No Obligation.

5.1 No Licence. All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants any licence under any patent, copyright or other intellectual property right.

5.2 No Obligation to Proceed. Neither party is obliged to enter into any further agreement or to purchase or supply any castings. Any collaboration shall be set out in a separate written contract.

5.3 No Warranty. Confidential Information is provided "as is", without any warranty as to its accuracy or completeness.

6\. Remedies.

6.1 Injunctive Relief. Each party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which damages would not be an adequate remedy, and that the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

6.2 Export Control. Each party shall comply with applicable export control laws in handling technical data received under this Agreement, and shall not export or re-export it without any required licence.

7\. General.

7.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws rules, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in New York County.

7.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties concerning its subject matter and may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.

7.3 Counterparts. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
